# School identity assets

- `tiangong.png`: Tiangong University’s purple seal, from https://cdn.urongda.com/images/normal/medium/tiangong-university-logo-1024px.png . The school’s own https://www.tiangong.edu.cn/ header was checked to confirm identity; its opaque white-on-purple header graphic was not used.
- `uem-wordmark.png`: University of Emergency Management’s blue seal and wordmark, from https://www.futseyi.com/assets/images/education/UEM.png . The original file is retained; CSS shows only its left-hand seal.

Both source PNGs have native RGBA transparency and no opaque white pixels. No bitmap edits or generated/reconstructed crests are used. School marks identify educational history; rights remain with their respective institutions. A scoped CSS brightness adjustment keeps the original colored marks legible on the dark theme.
